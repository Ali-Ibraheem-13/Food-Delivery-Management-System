# 🍔 Food Delivery Management System

## 📌 Project Overview

The Food Delivery Management System is a Python-based application designed to manage food ordering and delivery operations efficiently.

The system allows customers to browse food items, place orders, make payments, and track their orders. It also manages delivery partners, offers, coupons, and order reports.

The main objective of this project is to simplify the food ordering process and improve the management of food delivery operations.

## 🚀 Features

- **Customer Management:** Manage customer details and orders.
- **Food Ordering:** Allow customers to place food orders.
- **Delivery Partner Management:** Manage delivery partner information.
- **Order Management:** Track and manage customer orders.
- **Payment Management:** Support Cash on Delivery (COD) and UPI payment options.
- **Offers and Coupons:** Apply available discounts and promotional offers.
- **Delivery Time:** Estimate delivery time based on order processing.
- **Reports:** Generate reports related to orders, payments, and deliveries.
